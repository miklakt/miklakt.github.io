About

I am a researcher in theoretical polymer physics. I defended my PhD at the Université de Pau et des Pays de l’Adour (UPPA), where I studied nanocolloid interactions with polymer brushes of different shapes.

My work combines analytical models and numerical simulations to understand how molecular architecture affects soft-matter behavior. I use SCF methods in the strong-stretching limit and the Scheutjens–Fleer lattice approach, and I also work with molecular dynamics and Monte Carlo simulations.
